Endocrinology Services:
Comprehensive Care for Hormonal Health
At Sri Lakshmi Speciality Hospital, we offer a full spectrum of endocrinology services to diagnose, treat, and manage a variety of hormonal and metabolic conditions. Our expert endocrinologists provide personalized care tailored to each patient’s unique needs. Our services include:
Key Gynecological Services:
Diabetes Management:
Comprehensive care for type 1 and type 2 diabetes, including insulin therapy, continuous glucose monitoring, and patient education.
Thyroid Disorders:
Diagnosis and treatment of thyroid conditions such as hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, thyroid nodules, and thyroid cancer.
Osteoporosis and Bone Health:
Evaluation and treatment of osteoporosis and other metabolic bone diseases to prevent fractures and maintain bone strength.
Adrenal Disorders:
Management of adrenal gland disorders including Addison’s disease, Cushing’s syndrome, and adrenal tumors.
Pituitary Disorders:
Specialized care for pituitary gland conditions such as pituitary tumors, acromegaly, and hypopituitarism.
Reproductive Endocrinology:
Treatment of hormonal imbalances affecting fertility and reproductive health in both men and women.
Advanced Diagnostic Services:
Precision and Accuracy
Accurate diagnosis is essential for effective treatment. Our endocrinology department is equipped with the latest diagnostic tools and technology to provide precise and timely identification of endocrine conditions. Our advanced diagnostic services include:
Key Obstetrics Services:
Hormonal Testing:
Comprehensive blood tests to measure hormone levels and identify imbalances.
Ultrasound Imaging:
High-resolution ultrasound for detailed examination of the thyroid, adrenal, and other endocrine glands.
Fine-Needle Aspiration Biopsy:
Minimally invasive procedure to evaluate thyroid nodules and other glandular abnormalities.
Bone Density Testing (DEXA Scan):
Advanced imaging to assess bone health and diagnose osteoporosis.
Dynamic Endocrine Testing:
Specialized tests to evaluate the function of the adrenal and pituitary glands.

When should I see an endocrinologist?
You should consider seeing an endocrinologist if you have symptoms or a diagnosis of a hormone-related disorder, or if you are not responding well to initial treatments.
How is diabetes diagnosed and managed?
Diabetes is diagnosed through blood tests measuring glucose levels. Management includes blood sugar monitoring, lifestyle changes, medication, and insulin therapy as needed.
What is the role of hormones in the body?
Hormones are chemical messengers produced by glands that regulate various bodily functions, including metabolism, growth, reproduction, and mood.
Is hormonal imbalance the same as an endocrine disorder?
Hormonal imbalance can be a symptom of an endocrine disorder, but not all hormonal imbalances indicate a specific disorder.
Can hormone disorders cause weight gain or loss?
Yes, certain hormone disorders can lead to weight changes. For example, hypothyroidism may cause weight gain, while hyperthyroidism may lead to weight loss.
Are endocrine disorders treatable?
Many endocrine disorders are treatable or manageable with appropriate medical intervention and lifestyle modifications.
Can stress affect hormone levels?
Yes, chronic stress can impact hormone levels and may contribute to certain endocrine disorders.
