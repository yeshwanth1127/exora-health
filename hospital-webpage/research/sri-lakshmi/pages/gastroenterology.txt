You should see a gastroenterologist if you experience persistent digestive symptoms, abdominal pain, changes in bowel habits, or have a family history of GI disorders.
What can I do to prevent GERD and heartburn?
To reduce GERD symptoms, avoid trigger foods, eat smaller meals, avoid lying down after eating, and maintain a healthy weight.
Is colonoscopy painful?
Colonoscopy is typically performed under sedation, so most patients do not feel pain during the procedure.
Can liver cirrhosis be reversed?
Early-stage liver cirrhosis may be reversible if the underlying cause is treated. However, advanced cirrhosis is generally irreversible, and management focuses on preventing further damage and complications.
What is gluten, and why is it harmful in celiac disease?
Gluten is a protein found in wheat, barley, and rye. In celiac disease, gluten triggers an autoimmune response that damages the small intestine lining, leading to malabsorption and other symptoms.
Are there dietary restrictions for IBD or IBS?
Dietary restrictions may vary depending on the individual and the specific condition. A gastroenterologist or registered dietitian can provide personalized dietary advice
