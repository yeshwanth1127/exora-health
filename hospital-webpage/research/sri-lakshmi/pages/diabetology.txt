Understanding Diabetes
Empowering You to Live Well with Diabetes
At Sri Lakshmi Specialty Hospital, we understand the challenges of living with diabetes and the importance of managing the condition effectively. Our team of experienced diabetologists, endocrinologists, and diabetes educators work together to provide comprehensive care and support to individuals with diabetes, empowering them to lead fulfilling lives.
Key Aspects of Diabetes Management:
Education and Counseling:
Comprehensive education on diabetes management, including lifestyle modifications, diet, exercise, and medication management.
Regular Monitoring:
Routine monitoring of blood glucose levels, blood pressure, cholesterol, and other relevant parameters to assess diabetes control and prevent complications.
Personalized Treatment Plans:
Individualized treatment plans tailored to each patient’s unique needs, preferences, and health goals.
Prevention and Management of Complications:
Proactive measures to prevent and manage diabetes-related complications, such as neuropathy, nephropathy, retinopathy, and cardiovascular disease.

Specialized care and support for pregnant women with gestational diabetes to ensure optimal pregnancy outcomes for both mother and baby.

What is diabetes?
Diabetes is a chronic condition in which the body has difficulty regulating blood sugar (glucose). There are two main types: type 1 diabetes, which is an autoimmune condition where the body's immune system attacks the insulin-producing cells in the pancreas, and type 2 diabetes, which occurs when the body becomes resistant to insulin or does not produce enough insulin.
What are the symptoms of diabetes?
Common symptoms of diabetes include frequent urination, excessive thirst, unexplained weight loss, increased hunger, fatigue, and slow wound healing. However, some people with type 2 diabetes may have mild or no symptoms, leading to undiagnosed diabetes.
Can diabetes be prevented?
Type 1 diabetes cannot be prevented as it is an autoimmune condition. However, type 2 diabetes can often be prevented or delayed through lifestyle modifications such as healthy eating, regular exercise, and maintaining a healthy weight.
How often should I check my blood sugar levels?
The frequency of blood sugar monitoring depends on the type of diabetes, the treatment plan, and the healthcare provider's recommendations. Some patients may need to monitor several times a day, while others may require less frequent monitoring.

Can diabetes be cured?
As of now, there is no cure for diabetes. However, with proper management and lifestyle changes, many people with diabetes can lead healthy and fulfilling lives.
Is it safe for women with diabetes to get pregnant?
With careful planning and proper medical Supervision, women with diabetes can have healthy pregnancies. Pre-pregnancy counseling and close monitoring during pregnancy are crucial to ensure optimal outcomes for both the mother and the baby.
Can I still eat my favorite foods if I have diabetes?
Yes, you can enjoy your favorite foods in moderation. A balanced diet that includes a variety of foods and portion control is key to managing blood sugar levels.
How can I prevent complications from diabetes?
To prevent complications, it's essential to keep blood sugar levels under control, attend regular medical check-ups, follow your healthcare provider's recommendations, and adopt a healthy lifestyle.
