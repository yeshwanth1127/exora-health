When should I see an ENT specialist?
You should see an ENT specialist if you experience persistent ear pain, hearing loss, chronic sinus problems, voice changes, or swallowing difficulties.
Can allergies cause ear problems?
Yes, allergies can lead to ear problems, such as Eustachian tube dysfunction, which may cause ear pressure and hearing difficulties.
Is tonsillitis contagious?
Yes, tonsillitis caused by a viral or bacterial infection can be contagious and spread through respiratory droplets.
What causes vertigo?
Vertigo can be caused by inner ear disorders like benign paroxysmal positional vertigo (BPPV) or Meniere's disease, among others.
Can sinusitis be chronic?
Yes, sinusitis can become chronic if symptoms persist for more than 12 weeks despite appropriate medical treatment.
Is hearing loss permanent?
Hearing loss can be permanent in some cases, especially if it is due to age-related factors or certain types of inner ear damage.
What can I do to prevent ear infections?
To reduce the risk of ear infections, avoid inserting foreign objects into the ear canal and keep the ears dry after swimming or bathing.
