Cardiac Cathlab
Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital is proud to offer a state-of-the-art Cardiac Catheterization Laboratory (Cath Lab), a cornerstone of our comprehensive cardiovascular care. Our Cath Lab is equipped with cutting-edge technology and staffed by a team of highly skilled cardiologists, nurses, and technicians dedicated to diagnosing and treating a wide range of heart conditions with precision and care
Our Cath Lab is equipped with the latest digital imaging systems that provide high-resolution, real-time images of the heart and blood vessels. This advanced imaging technology enables our cardiologists to accurately diagnose cardiovascular conditions and guide precise treatment procedures.

The Cath Lab offers a wide range of diagnostic
and interventional procedures, including coronary angiography, angioplasty, stent placement, electrophysiological studies, and ablation therapy. These procedures help in diagnosing blockages, evaluating heart function, and treating arrhythmias and other cardiac conditions.
The procedures performed in our Cath Lab are minimally invasive
involving small incisions and the use of catheters. This approach reduces the risk of complications, minimizes pain and scarring, and promotes faster recovery times for patients.
Our Cath Lab is staffed
by a team of experienced cardiologists, interventional radiologists, cardiac nurses, and technicians who work collaboratively to provide the highest level of care. Their expertise ensures that patients receive accurate diagnoses and effective treatments tailored to their specific needs.
The Cath Lab is an integral part of our emergency cardiac care services
available 24/7 to handle urgent cases such as heart attacks. Our rapid response team is trained to perform life-saving procedures, such as primary angioplasty, to quickly restore blood flow to the heart and minimize damage.
At Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital
our Cardiac Cath Lab represents our unwavering commitment to excellence in cardiovascular care. By integrating advanced technology, skilled professionals, and a patient-focused approach, we strive to deliver the highest standard of cardiac care to our community.
Our Cardiac Cath Lab not only enhances
our ability to diagnose and treat heart conditions effectively but also underscores our dedication to advancing heart health and saving lives. With a focus on continuous improvement and innovation, Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital remains at the forefront of cardiovascular medicine, ensuring that our patients receive the best possible care for their heart health.
