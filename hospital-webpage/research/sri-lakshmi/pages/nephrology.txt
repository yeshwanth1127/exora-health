Comprehensive Nephrology Services
Our Commitment to Kidney Health and Wellness at Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital
At Sri Lakshmi Specialty Hospital, we offer a wide range of nephrology services to address the diverse needs of individuals with kidney-related conditions. Whether you are dealing with kidney disease, hypertension, or other renal disorders, our team is here to provide expert diagnosis, innovative treatments, and personalized care to help you achieve and maintain optimal kidney health.
Key Nephrology Services Include:
Nephrology Consultations:
Comprehensive evaluations by experienced nephrologists to diagnose and manage a variety of kidney-related conditions, including chronic kidney disease (CKD), acute kidney injury (AKI), glomerulonephritis, and electrolyte disorders.
Dialysis Services:
Comprehensive dialysis services, including hemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis, for patients with advanced kidney failure or end-stage renal disease (ESRD), performed in our state-of-the-art dialysis center by skilled nephrology nurses and technicians.
Kidney Transplantation:
Evaluation, preparation, and post-transplant care for patients undergoing kidney transplantation, including pre-transplant assessments, donor compatibility testing, and long-term transplant management.
Hypertension Management:
Specialized care for patients with hypertension (high blood pressure) and its associated complications, including renal hypertension, with a focus on blood pressure control and kidney protection.
Electrolyte and Acid-Base Disorders:
Diagnosis and treatment of electrolyte imbalances, such as hyperkalemia, hyponatremia, and metabolic acidosis, to restore normal kidney function and prevent complications.
Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Management:
Comprehensive management of CKD at all stages, including lifestyle modifications, medication therapy, and nutritional counseling, to slow disease progression and improve quality of life
Acute Kidney Injury (AKI) Care:
Rapid diagnosis and treatment of AKI, including supportive measures and renal replacement therapy (dialysis), to prevent further kidney damage and promote recovery.
Pediatric Nephrology:
Specialized care for children with kidney-related conditions, including congenital anomalies, urinary tract infections, nephrotic syndrome, and hereditary kidney disorders, to ensure optimal kidney health and growth.
Specialized Kidney Cares
Expertise Across a Range of Kidney Conditions
at Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital
Sri Lakshmi Specialty Hospital is home to a team of highly skilled nephrologists with expertise in diagnosing and treating a wide range of kidney-related conditions. Whether you are dealing with a common kidney disorder or a complex renal issue, our specialists are here to provide expert care and personalized treatment options to meet your unique needs.
Key Kidney Specialties Include:
Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD):
Comprehensive evaluation and management of CKD at all stages, including conservative measures, medication therapy, and renal replacement therapy (dialysis or transplant) for advanced disease.
Acute Kidney Injury (AKI):
Rapid diagnosis and treatment of AKI, including identification and correction of underlying causes, supportive care, and renal replacement therapy (dialysis) when necessary, to prevent further kidney damage and promote recovery.
Glomerular Disorders:
Evaluation and management of glomerular diseases, including glomerulonephritis, lupus nephritis, IgA nephropathy, and membranous nephropathy, with a focus on disease-specific therapies and renal protection strategies.
Polycystic Kidney Disease (PKD):
Specialized care for patients with PKD, including surveillance imaging, blood pressure management, and treatment of complications such as cyst infection, kidney stones, and hypertension.
Specialized care for patients with PKD, including surveillance imaging, blood pressure management, and treatment of complications such as cyst infection, kidney stones, and hypertension.
Diabetic Kidney Disease:
Comprehensive management of diabetic nephropathy, including glycemic control, blood pressure management, and renoprotective therapies, to slow the progression of kidney damage and reduce the risk of complications.
Hypertensive Kidney Disease:
Targeted treatment of hypertensive nephropathy, including blood pressure control, renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS) blockade, and lifestyle modifications, to preserve kidney function and prevent cardiovascular complications.
Kidney Stone Disease:
Evaluation and management of kidney stones, including medical therapy, dietary modifications, and minimally invasive procedures (such as lithotripsy or ureteroscopy) for stone removal and prevention of recurrence
Hereditary Kidney Disorders:
Genetic counseling, screening, and management of hereditary kidney diseases, including autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease (ADPKD), Alport syndrome, and Fabry disease, to optimize renal health and genetic counseling for at-risk individuals and families.

Key Nephrological Treatments and Technologies
Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy (CRRT): Advanced renal replacement therapy techniques, including continuous venovenous hemofiltration (CVVH) and continuous venovenous hemodialysis (CVVHD), for critically ill patients with AKI or ESRD.
Home Dialysis Programs: Comprehensive programs for home hemodialysis (HHD) and peritoneal dialysis (PD), offering patients the flexibility to receive dialysis treatments in the comfort of their own homes, with ongoing support from our nephrology team.
Plasmapheresis: Therapeutic plasma exchange (TPE) procedures for the treatment of autoimmune kidney diseases, such as lupus nephritis and anti-glomerular basement membrane (anti-GBM) disease, to remove harmful antibodies and toxins from the bloodstream.
Kidney Biopsy: Minimally invasive procedures, such as percutaneous kidney biopsy, for the diagnosis and management of kidney disorders, allowing for precise histopathological evaluation and treatment planning.
Immunosuppressive Therapy: Tailored immunosuppressive regimens for kidney transplant recipients, including induction therapy, maintenance immunosuppression, and treatment of rejection episodes, to prevent graft rejection and optimize transplant outcomes.

What function do the kidneys provide in the body?
In order to create urine, the kidneys filter waste materials and extra fluid from the blood. They also assist in controlling blood pressure and electrolyte levels.
What signs and symptoms of renal illness are typical?
Fatigue, edema, changes in urine output or appearance, chronic itching, nausea, and appetite changes are some of the common signs of kidney disease.
Kidney disease: is it preventable?
By controlling underlying illnesses including diabetes and hypertension, leading a healthy lifestyle, and limiting the use of over-the-counter painkillers, kidney disease can occasionally be avoided or postponed.

How frequently should my kidney function be evaluated?
Regular renal function testing is recommended for people who have conditions like diabetes or hypertension that increase their risk of kidney disease. Based on your health situation, your doctor can decide on the right frequency.
Is renal illness curable?
The fundamental reason affects whether kidney illness may be cured. While certain forms of kidney illness can be managed or cured, others may develop to the point where they are irreversible and call for dialysis or transplantation.
How long does it take to recover from a kidney transplant?
Individuals with kidney transplants recover at different rates. Patients typically require a few days to a week in the hospital, as well as several weeks of recuperation and follow-up care.
Can someone with renal illness lead a normal life?
Many people with renal illness can enjoy happy lives with the right care and lifestyle changes. The course of treatment, however, will be determined by the kidney disease's stage and severity.
