Is plastic surgery only for aesthetic purposes?
No, plastic surgery includes both aesthetic (cosmetic) procedures and reconstructive procedures aimed at restoring function and appearance.
What is the ideal age for plastic surgery?
The appropriate age for plastic surgery varies depending on the procedure and the patient's specific circumstances. Cosmetic procedures are generally performed on adults, while reconstructive surgeries may be performed on children and adults.
Are there any risks associated with plastic surgery?
All surgical procedures carry inherent risks, such as infection, bleeding, and adverse reactions to anesthesia. Plastic surgeons take extensive precautions to minimize these risks.
