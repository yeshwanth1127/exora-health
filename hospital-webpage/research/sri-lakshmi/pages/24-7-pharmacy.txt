24/7 Pharmacy
Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital’s 24/7 Pharmacy is a vital part of our commitment to providing comprehensive healthcare services. Understanding the critical role that timely medication plays in patient care, our pharmacy operates nonstop to ensure that patients and their families have access to necessary medications at any hour of the day or night.
Our 24/7 Pharmacy is staffed by a team of experienced pharmacists and pharmacy technicians who are dedicated to delivering accurate, efficient, and compassionate service. These professionals are available to provide expert advice on medication usage, potential side effects, and drug interactions, ensuring that patients fully understand their prescriptions and how to take them safely.
