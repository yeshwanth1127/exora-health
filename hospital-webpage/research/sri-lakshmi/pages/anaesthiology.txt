What is anaesthesia, and how does it work?
Anaesthesia is a medical technique used to induce a state of controlled unconsciousness or insensitivity to pain during surgical and medical procedures. It can be administered through various methods, including inhalation, injection, or regional nerve blocks. The primary goal of anesthesia is to ensure patient comfort and safety during the procedure.
Is anesthesia safe?
Yes, modern anesthesia techniques are generally safe. Our experienced and certified anesthesiologists follow strict safety protocols and continuously monitor vital signs throughout the procedure. They tailor the anesthesia plan to each patient's unique medical history and needs, minimizing risks and complications.
How do I prepare for anesthesia?
Before your procedure, our team will conduct a thorough preoperative assessment. Follow any fasting or medication instructions provided to you by our medical staff to ensure the effectiveness and safety of anesthesia. Be sure to disclose any allergies, medical conditions, or medications you are currently taking during the preoperative evaluation.
What are the different types of anesthesia?
There are three main types of anesthesia:
General Anesthesia: You will be in a state of complete unconsciousness and feel no pain during the procedure.
Regional Anesthesia: Specific regions of your body will be numbed while you remain conscious or sedated.
Monitored Anesthesia Care (MAC): You will receive sedation and pain relief while remaining conscious during the procedure.
Will I feel pain after the surgery?
Our anesthesiologists work closely with the surgical team to ensure adequate pain management after the procedure. Various pain relief techniques, such as local anesthetics, nerve blocks, and pain medications, will be used to minimize discomfort during the recovery process.
Can children receive anesthesia safely?
Yes, our Anesthesiology Department includes pediatric anesthesia specialists who are highly trained in providing safe and effective anesthesia for children. Their expertise ensures that children receive age-appropriate care and attention during surgical procedures.
What if I have special medical conditions or allergies?
Our team will conduct a comprehensive preoperative assessment to evaluate your medical history and any potential risks related to anesthesia. It is essential to inform us about any allergies, medical conditions, or medications you are currently taking so that we can tailor the anesthesia plan accordingly.
How long does anesthesia take to wear off?
The duration of anesthesia's effects varies depending on the type of anesthesia used and individual factors. Generally, the effects of general anesthesia wear off within a few hours after the procedure, but it may take longer for the effects of regional or local anesthesia to subside.
