Who We Are
Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital, KR Puram: Leading Healthcare Excellence
Welcome to Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital in KR Puram, Bangalore! Founded in 2002 by Dr. Sambashiva, our hospital is part of the renowned Sri Lakshmi Group of Hospitals. We are dedicated to making top-quality healthcare affordable and accessible to everyone in the KR Puram area.
Our 50-bed facility is equipped with the latest medical technologies and staffed by a team of expert doctors. We offer a wide range of specialized services, including: Heart Care, Emergency and Trauma Care, Kidney Care, Brain and Nerve Care, Men’s Health, General and Keyhole Surgery, Women’s Health
At Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital, our goal is to provide the best possible care in a comfortable and compassionate environment. Whether you need a routine check-up or advanced medical treatment, our experienced specialists are here to help.
Come experience the difference at Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital, where we are committed to your health and well-being!

Our Value
Upholding Excellence, Empathy, and Integrity in Every Patient Journey
Our Vision
At Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital, KR Puram, our vision is to transform healthcare by making it affordable and within reach for everyone. We are dedicated to bringing the latest medical technologies and top-notch facilities to our community, ensuring that every person receives the best care possible.
Our Mission
Our mission is to make advanced healthcare services and technology accessible and affordable for all. We believe that everyone deserves excellent medical care, which is why we have a team of the most experienced and caring doctors ready to serve you. At Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital, we are committed to improving the health and well-being of our community, one patient at a time.
Founder's Message
Greetings,
My journey in healthcare began with a simple belief: that everyone deserves access to quality medical care, regardless of their circumstances. Having witnessed the gaps in facilities and technology firsthand during my early days at a government hospital, I was inspired to make a difference.
In 2002, Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital was born out of this vision—to bring advanced healthcare closer to home in KR Puram. Beyond treatment, our mission extends to supporting our community. We’ve helped countless families with minimal medical bills and conducted outreach programs across Karnataka to promote health awareness.
Thank you for being a part of our journey. Together, let’s continue to provide compassionate care and make a positive impact on lives.
Warm regards,
Dr. Sambashiva
Founder, Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital
