Do I need a doctor's referral to see a physiotherapist?
In many countries, you can directly access physiotherapy services without a doctor's referral. However, insurance policies and regulations may vary.
How long does a physiotherapy session typically last?
Physiotherapy sessions usually last between 30 minutes to an hour, depending on the treatment plan and the patient's needs.
Will physiotherapy be painful?
Physiotherapy aims to alleviate pain and promote healing. While some techniques may cause mild discomfort during treatment, it should not be excessively painful.
How many physiotherapy sessions will I need?
The number of sessions required depends on the individual's condition, severity of the problem, and response to treatment. The physiotherapist will determine the appropriate treatment plan.
Can physiotherapy prevent surgery?
In some cases, physiotherapy can help manage and improve certain conditions, potentially reducing the need for surgery. However, it varies depending on the specific situation.
Is physiotherapy only for adults?
Physiotherapy is beneficial for people of all ages, including infants, children, and older adults.
What should I wear to a physiotherapy session?
Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows easy access to the affected area for assessment and treatment.
