24/7 IPD & OPD
24/7 Inpatient Department (IPD)
Our IPD services provide around-the-clock care for patients requiring hospitalization. The IPD is equipped with advanced medical facilities and comfortable patient rooms, designed to create a healing environment. Our multidisciplinary team of doctors, nurses, and support staff work tirelessly to deliver personalized care and ensure the best possible outcomes for our patients.
We offer specialized inpatient services across various medical disciplines, including cardiology, neurology, orthopaedics, gastroenterology, and more. Our IPD is supported by an in-house critical care unit, intensive care unit (ICU), and high-dependency unit (HDU), equipped with cutting-edge technology to manage severe and life-threatening conditions. Our team follows rigorous protocols and leverages advanced monitoring systems to provide continuous care and swiftly respond to any changes in a patient’s condition.

24/7 Outpatient Department (OPD)
Our OPD services are designed to offer convenient access to medical consultations, diagnostic services, and treatment procedures without the need for hospitalization. The 24/7 availability of our OPD ensures that patients can seek medical attention at any time, accommodating their schedules and addressing urgent healthcare needs.
Patients can access a wide range of specialist consultations in our OPD, including general medicine, paediatrics, gynecology, dermatology, and more. Our experienced physicians and healthcare professionals are dedicated to delivering high-quality, patient-centered care. The OPD is equipped with modern diagnostic tools and facilities, allowing for prompt and accurate diagnosis and treatment.
Our 24/7 OPD also supports preventive healthcare through routine check-ups, health screenings, and vaccinations, helping patients maintain their health and well-being.
