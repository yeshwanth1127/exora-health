Dialysis Services:
Comprehensive Care for Kidney Health
At Sri Lakshmi Speciality Hospital, we offer a full spectrum of dialysis services to support patients with kidney disease and ensure their overall well-being. Our expert nephrologists provide personalized care tailored to each patient’s unique needs. Our services include:
Key Gynecological Services:
Hemodialysis:
Effective removal of waste and excess fluid from the blood using advanced dialysis machines.
Peritoneal Dialysis:
A home-based treatment that uses the peritoneum in the abdomen as a membrane to filter blood.
Continuous Ambulatory Peritoneal Dialysis (CAPD):
A form of peritoneal dialysis that allows patients to perform exchanges manually throughout the day.
Automated Peritoneal Dialysis (APD):
A nighttime peritoneal dialysis treatment performed by a machine while the patient sleeps.

When is dialysis necessary?
Dialysis is necessary when the kidneys lose their ability to adequately filter waste and fluids from the blood, resulting in a dangerous buildup of toxins in the body.
Is dialysis permanent?
For individuals with ESRD, dialysis is a lifelong treatment unless they receive a kidney transplant.
How long does a dialysis session last?
Hemodialysis sessions typically last around 3 to 4 hours and are usually required three times a week. Peritoneal dialysis involves daily exchanges of dialysis fluid.
Can I travel while on dialysis?
Yes, dialysis patients can travel, but it requires some planning and coordination with healthcare providers to ensure access to dialysis facilities at the travel destination.
Can I eat or drink during dialysis?
During hemodialysis, patients may be restricted from eating or drinking due to the potential risk of fluid overload. Peritoneal dialysis allows for more flexibility in diet and fluid intake.
Are there any side effects of dialysis?
Dialysis can cause side effects like low blood pressure, muscle cramps, nausea, and fatigue. However, these can often be managed with proper medical care.
Can a kidney transplant eliminate the need for dialysis?
Yes, a successful kidney transplant can restore kidney function, eliminating the need for dialysis.
Does dialysis cure kidney disease?
Dialysis does not cure kidney disease but serves as a life-sustaining treatment by performing the function of the kidneys when they are no longer able to do so.
