What are the common symptoms of respiratory diseases?
Common symptoms include cough, shortness of breath, chest pain, wheezing, and coughing up blood.
Is COPD reversible?
While COPD is not reversible, early diagnosis and appropriate management can slow down its progression and improve quality of life.
Can lung cancer be prevented?
Although not all lung cancers can be prevented, avoiding tobacco smoke and reducing exposure to environmental toxins can significantly lower the risk.
Are there vaccines to prevent respiratory infections?
Yes, vaccines are available for certain respiratory infections like influenza and pneumonia. It's essential to get vaccinated, especially for individuals at higher risk.
How can I keep my lungs healthy?
Regular exercise, avoiding smoking and second-hand smoke, maintaining good indoor air quality, and getting vaccinated can help keep your lungs healthy.
What are the early warning signs of asthma worsening?
Early warning signs include increased use of rescue inhalers, frequent coughing at night, and decreased lung function.
Is pulmonary rehabilitation suitable for all respiratory conditions?
Pulmonary rehabilitation can benefit many respiratory conditions, but its suitability is assessed on a case-by-case basis.
Can lung diseases be hereditary?
Yes, certain lung diseases like cystic fibrosis have a genetic basis and can be inherited from parents.
