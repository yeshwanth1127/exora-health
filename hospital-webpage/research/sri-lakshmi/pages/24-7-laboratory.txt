24/7 Laboratory
24/7 Laboratory
Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital’s 24/7 Laboratory is a cornerstone of our commitment to providing comprehensive, round-the-clock healthcare services. Our state-of-the-art laboratory operates continuously, ensuring that critical diagnostic services are always available to support timely and accurate patient care.
Staffed by a team of highly trained and experienced laboratory professionals, including pathologists, lab technicians, and support staff, our 24/7 Laboratory is equipped with advanced diagnostic equipment and technologies. This enables us to conduct a wide range of tests, from routine blood work and urinalysis to complex biochemical, microbiological, and histopathological analyses.

The 24/7 availability of our laboratory
services is particularly crucial in emergency and trauma care situations, where rapid diagnostic results can significantly impact treatment decisions and patient outcomes. Our lab team works in close collaboration with the hospital's emergency department and medical specialists to ensure that urgent test results are delivered promptly, facilitating swift and effective medical interventions.
our 24/7 Laboratory
At Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital, our 24/7 Laboratory ensures that reliable and timely diagnostic services are always within reach, providing essential support to our medical team and peace of mind to our patients. By delivering precise and prompt diagnostic results, we help pave the way for effective treatment and better health outcomes for all.
In addition to immediate medical attention
