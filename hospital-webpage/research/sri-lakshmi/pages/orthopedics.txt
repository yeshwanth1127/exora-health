Comprehensive Orthopedic Care
Restoring Function and Quality of Life
At Sri Lakshmi Specialty Hospital, we offer a wide range of orthopedic services to address the diverse needs of individuals with bone and joint conditions. Whether you are dealing with an acute injury, chronic pain, or degenerative condition, our team is here to provide expert diagnosis, innovative treatments, and compassionate care to help you regain mobility and improve your quality of life.
Key Orthopedic Services Include:
Orthopedic Consultations:
Comprehensive evaluations by experienced orthopedic specialists to diagnose and treat musculoskeletal conditions.
Joint Replacement Surgery:
Advanced surgical procedures, including total knee replacement, total hip replacement, and shoulder replacement, to relieve pain and restore function.
Arthroscopic Surgery:
Minimally invasive procedures to diagnose and treat joint problems, such as torn ligaments, cartilage damage, and joint inflammation.
Fracture Care:
Prompt evaluation and treatment of fractures, including casting, splinting, and surgical intervention when necessary.
Sports Medicine
Specialized care for sports-related injuries, including ligament tears, tendonitis, and stress fractures, to help athletes return to peak performance.
Spine Care:
Comprehensive evaluation and treatment of spine conditions, including herniated discs, spinal stenosis, and degenerative disc disease.
Orthopedic Rehabilitation:
Customized rehabilitation programs to promote healing, restore strength and flexibility, and prevent recurrence of injuries.
Restoring Spinal Health and Function at Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital
Sri Lakshmi Specialty Hospital offers comprehensive spine care services, including diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation for a wide range of spinal conditions. Our multidisciplinary team of spine specialists utilizes advanced diagnostic techniques and innovative treatment modalities to address spine-related issues and help patients achieve optimal spinal health and function.
Key Spine Care Services Include:
Diagnostic Imaging:
Advanced imaging studies, including X-rays, MRI, and CT scans, for accurate diagnosis and evaluation of spinal conditions.
Non-Surgical Treatments:
Conservative management approaches, such as medication therapy, physical therapy, and spinal injections, to relieve pain and improve function.
Minimally Invasive Spine Surgery:
Innovative surgical techniques, such as microdiscectomy, laminectomy, and spinal fusion, performed through small incisions for reduced tissue damage and faster recovery.
Complex Spine Surgery:
Comprehensive surgical interventions for complex spinal disorders, deformities, and trauma, performed by skilled spine surgeons with expertise in advanced techniques.
Spinal Rehabilitation:
Customized rehabilitation programs designed to strengthen the muscles surrounding the spine, improve flexibility, and enhance overall spinal function.

Key Orthopedic Treatments and Technologies
Computer-Assisted Surgery: Precision-guided surgical techniques for improved accuracy and outcomes in joint replacement and spine surgery.
Robotic-Assisted Surgery: State-of-the-art robotic systems for enhanced precision and control in orthopedic procedures, leading to better patient outcomes.

What can I do to prevent orthopedic injuries?
To reduce the risk of orthopedic injuries, maintain a healthy weight, participate in regular exercise, practice proper body mechanics, warm-up before physical activity, and use appropriate protective gear during sports.
How can I manage chronic joint pain?
Managing chronic joint pain may involve a combination of medications, physical therapy, lifestyle modifications, and pain management techniques. Consult with an orthopedic specialist to discuss the best approach for your specific condition.
Are orthopedic conditions only related to bones?
No, orthopedic conditions can also affect muscles, ligaments, tendons, and other soft tissues in addition to bones and joints.
Can children with orthopedic conditions be treated differently than adults?
Yes, pediatric orthopedic conditions may require specialized approaches due to growing bones and unique musculoskeletal considerations in children.
