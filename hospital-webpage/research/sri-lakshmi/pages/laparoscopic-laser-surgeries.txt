Laparoscopic & Laser Surgeries
Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital is at the forefront of offering advanced Laparoscopic and Laser Surgery services, providing minimally invasive surgical options that offer numerous benefits over traditional open surgeries. These cutting-edge techniques are performed by skilled surgeons using state-of-the-art equipment, ensuring precision, minimal discomfort, and faster recovery for patients.
Laparoscopic procedures require smaller incisions compared to traditional open surgery, which reduces trauma to surrounding tissues, minimizes scarring, and accelerates healing.
