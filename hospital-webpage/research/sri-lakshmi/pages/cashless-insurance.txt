Cashless Treatments at Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital in KR Puram, Bangalore
Welcome to Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital, your trusted partner in health and wellness in Bangalore. We are proud to introduce our comprehensive cashless treatment and insurance facility, making healthcare accessible and hassle-free for everyone. Whether you have private or government insurance, our streamlined process ensures that you receive top-notch medical care without the stress of upfront payments.
Government Insurance and Schemes:
For the first time in Bangalore, Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital offers super speciality services under various government schemes. We are committed to providing quality healthcare to all, and our tie-ups with the following government insurance schemes reflect this dedication:
Advantages :
Arogya Sri
ABARK (Aarogyasri Bhadratha And Rachabanda Kalyana Lakshmi)
ESI (Employees' State Insurance)
And many more…
These collaborations allow patients to receive advanced medical treatments and services without worrying about the financial burden. Our experienced team handles the paperwork and approvals, so you can focus on your recovery.
Private Insurance:
In addition to government schemes, Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital has direct tie-ups with all major private insurance companies and TPAs (Third-Party Administrators). This extensive network simplifies the cashless treatment process, making it easier and more convenient for patients with private insurance. Our affiliations include, but are not limited to:
Advantages :
Bajaj Allianz
ICICI Lombard
Max Bupa Health Insurance

And many more…
Our dedicated insurance desk is here to assist you with all your insurance-related queries and ensure a smooth, cashless hospitalization experience.
