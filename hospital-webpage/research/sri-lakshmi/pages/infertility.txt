Infertility Services:
Comprehensive Care for Your Fertility Journey
At Sri Lakshmi Speciality Hospital, we offer a full spectrum of infertility services to diagnose, treat, and manage fertility issues in both men and women. Our expert fertility specialists provide personalized care tailored to each patient’s unique needs. Our services include:
Key Gynecological Services:
Diagnostic Services:
Comprehensive fertility evaluations including hormonal tests, semen analysis, and imaging studies to identify the underlying causes of infertility.
Ovulation Induction:
Medications and monitoring to stimulate ovulation and enhance the chances of conception.
Intrauterine Insemination (IUI):
A minimally invasive procedure that places sperm directly into the uterus to increase the likelihood of fertilization.
In Vitro Fertilization (IVF):
Advanced assisted reproductive technology involving the retrieval of eggs, fertilization in the laboratory, and embryo transfer to the uterus.
Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI):
A specialized form of IVF where a single sperm is injected directly into an egg to facilitate fertilization.
Donor Programs:
Egg, sperm, and embryo donation programs to assist individuals and couples facing specific fertility challenges.
Contraceptive Counseling:
Guidance on the best contraceptive options for your lifestyle and needs.
Advanced Fertility Treatments:
Innovative Solutions for Better Outcomes
Our infertility department utilizes a variety of advanced treatments and techniques to ensure the best possible outcomes for our patients. Our innovative solutions include:
Key Obstetrics Services:
Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT):
Screening embryos for genetic abnormalities to enhance the chances of a healthy pregnancy.
Fertility Preservation:
Options such as egg, sperm, and embryo freezing for individuals and couples wishing to delay parenthood.
Laparoscopic and Hysteroscopic Surgery:
Minimally invasive surgeries to correct structural issues affecting fertility, such as fibroids, polyps, and endometriosis.

When should I seek help for infertility?
If you have been actively trying to conceive for a year without success (or six months if the woman is over 35), it's advisable to consult a fertility specialist.
Can stress cause infertility?
While stress can affect overall health, there is no definitive evidence that stress alone directly causes infertility. However, managing stress during the fertility journey is essential.
Is infertility only a female problem?
Infertility can result from male, female, or combined factors. Approximately one-third of infertility cases are attributed to female factors, one-third to male factors, and one-third to combined or unexplained causes.
Does age affect fertility?
Yes, female fertility declines with age, primarily due to a decrease in the number and quality of eggs. Male fertility may also be affected by age, although to a lesser extent.
Is fertility treatment always successful?
Success rates for fertility treatments vary depending on factors such as the underlying cause of infertility, the age of the individuals involved, and the specific treatment being used.
Are fertility treatments safe?
Fertility treatments are generally safe, but like any medical procedure, they carry some risks. It's essential to discuss potential risks and benefits with a fertility specialist.
Can lifestyle changes improve fertility?
Adopting a healthy lifestyle, including maintaining a balanced diet, regular exercise, managing stress, and avoiding smoking and excessive alcohol consumption, can positively impact fertility in some cases.
