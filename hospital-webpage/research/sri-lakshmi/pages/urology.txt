What does a urologist do?
A urologist is a medical doctor who specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the urinary tract and male reproductive system. They manage conditions such as kidney stones, urinary tract infections, prostate issues, and urological cancers.
When should I see a urologist?
You should consider seeing a urologist if you experience symptoms such as frequent urination, painful urination, blood in urine, kidney stones, erectile dysfunction, or concerns about male fertility.
How can I prevent kidney stones?
To reduce the risk of kidney stones, drink plenty of water, maintain a balanced diet, limit salt intake, and avoid excessive consumption of foods high in oxalate (e.g., spinach, nuts).
Are urinary tract infections (UTIs) more common in women?
Yes, UTIs are more common in women due to their shorter urethra, which allows bacteria to reach the bladder more easily. However, UTIs can occur in men as well.
Is prostate cancer curable?
Prostate cancer is often treatable, especially when detected early. Treatment options may include surgery, radiation therapy, hormone therapy, and active surveillance for low-risk cases.
What is the recovery time after urological surgery?
Recovery time varies depending on the type of surgery and the individual's health. Minimally invasive procedures typically have shorter recovery times than open surgeries.
Can urological conditions affect sexual health?
Yes, certain urological conditions, such as erectile dysfunction, Peyronie's disease, and prostate issues, can affect sexual health and function.
