10 Bedded ICU
Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital features a state-of-the-art 10-bedded Intensive Care Unit (ICU), designed to provide the highest level of care for critically ill patients. Our ICU is equipped with advanced medical technology and staffed by a dedicated team of healthcare professionals who are committed to delivering compassionate, round-the-clock care.
Each bed in our ICU is equipped with sophisticated monitoring systems that continuously track vital signs such as heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and respiratory rate. These systems enable our medical team to promptly detect and respond to any changes in a patient’s condition.

Our ICU features
the latest medical equipment, including ventilators, infusion pumps, dialysis machines, and defibrillators. This technology supports the comprehensive management of critically ill patients with complex medical needs.
The ICU is staffed by a multidisciplinary
