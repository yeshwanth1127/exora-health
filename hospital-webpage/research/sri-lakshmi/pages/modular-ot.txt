Modular OT
Modular OT
Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital features a state-of-the-art Modular Operating Theatre (OT), reflecting our commitment to delivering the highest standards of surgical care. Our Modular OT is designed with advanced technology and infrastructure to ensure a sterile, efficient, and safe environment for performing a wide range of surgical procedures.
The Modular OT is designed to maintain a highly sterile environment, critical for preventing infections during surgical procedures. The theatre is equipped with HEPA (High-Efficiency Particulate Air) filters and laminar airflow systems that control air quality and reduce the risk of airborne contamination.

Our OT is outfitted
with the latest surgical equipment and technology, including high-definition cameras, advanced imaging systems, and robotic-assisted surgery tools. These technologies enable our surgeons to perform complex and minimally invasive surgeries with precision and accuracy.
The modular design of our OT allows for flexibility and scalability
accommodating various types of surgeries and adapting to future technological advancements. This design also facilitates easy maintenance and quick repairs, minimizing downtime and ensuring continuous availability of the theatre.
The OT is integrated with sophisticated monitoring
and communication systems that enhance the efficiency and safety of surgical procedures. These systems allow for real-time monitoring of patient vitals, seamless communication between surgical teams, and immediate access to patient records and imaging results.
