10 Bedded Dialysis
Sri Lakshmi Super Speciality Hospital is equipped with a specialized 10-bedded Dialysis Unit dedicated to providing comprehensive renal care to patients requiring hemodialysis. Our Dialysis Unit is designed to deliver high-quality treatment in a comfortable and safe environment, ensuring optimal management of kidney-related conditions.
Our Dialysis Unit is equipped with state-of-the-art hemodialysis machines that utilize the latest technology to efficiently remove toxins and excess fluid from the blood. These machines are capable of delivering precise dialysis treatments tailored to each patient’s needs.

The Dialysis Unit is staffed
by a team of experienced nephrologists, dialysis nurses, and technicians who specialize in renal care. They monitor patients closely during dialysis sessions, ensuring safe and effective treatment outcomes.
We adhere to stringent infection control protocols
to prevent the spread of infections within the Dialysis Unit. This includes regular disinfection of equipment and facilities, as well as adherence to hand hygiene and personal protective measures.
