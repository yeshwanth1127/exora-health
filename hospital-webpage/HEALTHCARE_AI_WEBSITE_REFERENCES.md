# Healthcare AI website references

Reviewed: 2026-09-29. These are visual and content references for the new healthcare company website. The observations describe the public homepages as viewed on desktop; they are not assets or claims to reuse.

| Site | Reference | Distinctive design language | Content emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hyro | https://www.hyro.ai/ | Pale lavender background, deep navy type, saturated violet actions, a serif italic accent, and a cutout person/product scene beside the headline. | Patient access and staff workload; customer outcomes, responsible AI, agent capabilities, integrations. |
| Nabla | https://www.nabla.com/ | Full-bleed clinician/patient photography, dark green overlay, centered white type with a mint accent, restrained navigation. | Clinical workflow and human connection; scale, workflow breadth, EHR integration, security. |
| Ambience Healthcare | https://www.ambiencehealthcare.com/ | Warm off-white canvas, very large editorial serif headline, centered composition, violet button, and large clinical photography below the hero. | Clinician adoption and measurable financial outcomes; evidence by role and care setting. |
| Abridge | https://www.abridge.com/ | Warm documentary-style care photography filling the hero, simple white type over a dark image treatment, small orange actions. | Human moments of care; customer stories, clinical impact, platform and research. |
| Notable | https://www.notablehealth.com/ | Airy white space, precise blue typography and buttons, sparse floating product/interface panels rather than a dominant photograph. | Enterprise automation across access, revenue cycle, and care operations; workflow examples and results. |

## Shared pattern

1. **Lead with an outcome.** The hero names a problem or promised change in a short headline, followed by a plain explanation of who benefits.
2. **Give one primary action.** Demo/contact is visually dominant; video or customer stories are secondary.
3. **Show healthcare context immediately.** Most use real care imagery; Notable uses concrete product UI. Neither approach relies on generic abstract AI imagery.
4. **Earn trust in layers.** Customer names appear early, followed by specific stories, measured outcomes, clinical/technical evidence, integrations, and security or governance information.
5. **Use generous space and a controlled palette.** Large type, few colors, a strong dark text color, and one recognizable accent keep complex enterprise offerings approachable.
6. **Organize by buyer need.** Product/platform pages sit alongside solution pages for roles, workflows, or care settings.

## Direction to explore for our site

Use a calm, editorial base with a distinctive type pairing, one strong brand accent, generous spacing, and photography or product imagery that shows the actual service. Make the homepage sequence: clear outcome → product in context → who it serves → how it works → real evidence → trust/security → one clear contact action. Keep copy specific to the new company's actual product and audience. Customer logos, outcomes, compliance marks, and clinical claims should appear only when verified.

The current repository is an Avocado Health patient-facing prototype. These references are primarily enterprise healthcare AI sites, so their demo-led sales funnel and claims should be adapted to the new company's audience rather than copied into the existing clinic experience.
