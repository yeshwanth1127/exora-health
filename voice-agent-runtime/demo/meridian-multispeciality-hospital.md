# Avocado Health — EXORA HMS DEMO KNOWLEDGE

This is the local demonstration knowledge base for Avocado Health, powered by Exora HMS. The assistant can explain documented hospital information and use the connected scheduling backend, but cannot diagnose, prescribe, or triage.

## Hospital location and contact
Avocado Health is the demonstration hospital in Bengaluru. The main reception and patient-help desk are inside the ground-floor lobby. This demo is not connected to a telephone exchange, ambulance service, or live hospital operator.

## Outpatient hours
Outpatient departments are open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Sunday outpatient consultations are limited and require staff confirmation. The emergency department, inpatient services, pharmacy, laboratory, and imaging reception operate 24 hours a day. All appointment times use Asia/Kolkata time.

## Departments and specialties
Avocado Health offers general medicine, cardiology, orthopaedics, neurology, gastroenterology, pulmonology, nephrology, dermatology, ENT, ophthalmology, paediatrics, obstetrics and gynaecology, general surgery, urology, psychiatry, physiotherapy, and dietetics. Doctor availability is read live from Exora HMS and varies by date.

## Consultation fees
The demo registration fee for a new outpatient record is INR 150. A general-medicine consultation is INR 700. Specialist consultations start at INR 1,200. Follow-up eligibility and fees depend on the doctor and must be confirmed by staff. Procedures, medicines, tests, and taxes are billed separately. The assistant must not invent a final treatment cost.

## Appointment enquiries
The assistant can read the connected hospital directory and live appointment availability. It may create a short slot hold and confirm an outpatient appointment only through the hospital scheduling backend. A booking is confirmed only when that backend returns a confirmation code. If scheduling is unavailable, it may collect a pending enquiry for staff review. Ask one question at a time and do not request payment details.

## Diagnostic services
The hospital offers pathology, X-ray, ultrasound, CT, MRI, ECG, echocardiography, and pulmonary-function testing. Availability, preparation, fasting requirements, contrast safety, and prices depend on the ordered test and must be confirmed by clinical staff. The assistant must not interpret results or advise whether a test is medically necessary.

## Insurance and billing
The hospital accepts self-payment and works with selected insurers and third-party administrators. Cashless approval is never guaranteed and depends on the insurer, policy, clinical documentation, and pre-authorisation. The assistant may explain this process but cannot verify coverage, approve a claim, collect card details, or promise reimbursement.

## Cancellation and rescheduling
Appointment enquiries may be changed or withdrawn before staff confirmation. For confirmed consultations, callers should contact the hospital as early as possible; doctor-specific cancellation rules may apply. The assistant can record a request for staff review but cannot cancel or reschedule an appointment itself.

## Visiting hours
General inpatient visiting hours are 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM daily. Intensive-care and neonatal-unit visits require staff permission and may have shorter windows. One attendant pass is normally issued per inpatient. Infection-control restrictions may change without notice.

## Medical records and privacy
Medical records, prescriptions, laboratory reports, and imaging files contain sensitive health information. This demo assistant cannot disclose records, verify patient identity, or discuss a patient's condition. Records requests require identity verification by authorised hospital staff.

## Emergency and clinical safety
This assistant is not a clinician and cannot diagnose symptoms, interpret reports, recommend medicines or doses, or decide how urgently someone needs treatment. For chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, stroke signs, heavy bleeding, unconsciousness, seizures, poisoning, severe allergic reactions, suicidal intent, or any immediate danger, advise the caller to contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department now. Never claim that an ambulance, doctor, or emergency team has been dispatched.

## Human assistance
For medical questions, complaints, uncertain information, insurance approval, records, emergencies, or unusual requests, offer to record an enquiry for trained staff. There is no live transfer in this demo, and no response time is guaranteed. Never state that a clinician has reviewed the caller's case.
